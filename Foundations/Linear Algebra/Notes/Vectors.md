**Definition**: 
1. Arrows pointing in space, defined by length and direction (2D or 3D; physics def)
2. Anything where there is a notion of adding, multiplying (math def)
3. Ordered list of numbers used to define features (cs def)
    Ex. Square footage and price in a house would be defined as [2600,300000] which is != to [300000,2600]

* Think of all vectors as a dot sitting in the xy plane where the tail is on the origin
* <mark>vector addition</mark> : imagine putting the tail of one vector on the end of another and the vector created by those two vectors (starting from the origin) is the sum
    + Mathematically defined as [a,b] + [c,d] = [a+b, c+d]

* vectors can be noted as letters with unidirectional arrows on the top of them
* <mark>vector multiplication with scalars</mark> : stretch or shrink a vector, any number that scales a vector is called a *scalar* which is what most constants in linear algebra are reffered to as
    + Mathematical def: 2 * [a,b] = [2a, 2b]
